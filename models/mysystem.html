Design a controller for your project plant

Enter a first-order model measured from your project or taken from a cited datasheet. This page simulates a sampled PI/PID controller with RK4 and generates matching Arduino code.

First-order plantG(s) = K / (τs + 1)
Sampling and PWM0 ≤ u[k] ≤ 1
K
output gain / normalized PWM fraction
τ
time constant, seconds
Ts
digital sample interval, seconds

The output and reference are normalized to the scale 0–1. The simulation uses zero-order hold, conditional anti-windup and the discrete PID equation from P3.

How to identify your project model

  1. 1. Step inputPWMApply a known step fraction and log the sensor response
  2. 2. GainKChange in normalized steady output / change in PWM fraction
  3. 3. SpeedτTime to reach 63.2% of the measured output change
